Phylogenetic diversity

A phylogenetic tree was generated from a concatenated alignment of the eight giant-virus marker genes: individual marker alignments produced with MAFFT, trimmed with TrimAl, concatenated, and used for tree inference with IQ-TREE. Only viruses with at least five retained markers were included.
